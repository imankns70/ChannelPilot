# Channel behaviors

## Football

The Football channel publishes attractive, short Telegram posts about football news, transfers, injuries, suspensions, match results, interviews, club crises, national teams, and major football events. Publishing is **event-based**: content is collected and considered when a relevant event occurs.

Detailed policy: [Football channel rules](rules/football-channel-rules.md)

## Trend

The Trend channel covers trending and viral stories from different categories. It is **quality-gated**, with a higher acceptance threshold than routine collection. Content will usually require manual approval before publication.

Detailed policy: [Trend channel rules](rules/trend-channel-rules.md)

## Novel

The Novel channel publishes serial fiction. It is **schedule-based**, not event-based. Exactly one episode is published every night at a fixed time. Long novels must be split into episodes and released across multiple nights.

Detailed policy: [Novel channel rules](rules/novel-channel-rules.md)

Shared policy: [Scoring strategy](rules/scoring-strategy.md) and [publishing policy](rules/publishing-policy.md)
